My name is Sophia Park, welcome to my data blog!
I currently work as an Algorithm Engineer and previously as a Data Scientist. I am passionate about drawing insights from data, and communicating them in creative ways. My interests are in data visualization, understanding black boxes (model interpretation), and safety. If you are working in any of these areas, I would love to talk!

A little about me: I am from Korea, and have lived in both Korea and the United States. I hold a Bachelor's in Electrical Engineering from Duke and a Masters in Statistics from Harvard. I currently live in San Diego, roughly 8 hours from my twin sister. For fun, I like crafts, pilates, reading, cooking/baking, and food.
